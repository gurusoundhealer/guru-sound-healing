PAGE 05 · NEURO-ACOUSTIC PHYSICS

How sound supports relaxation

Sound healing relies on the physics of sound combined with the body's physiological response to vibration. When sound waves travel through the air and reach the body, they can interact with mechanical, sensory and neural processes.

FREQUENCY FOLLOWING RESPONSE

Neural Entrainment

Rhythmic, sustained sounds can influence the timing of neural activity and may support a shift from highly alert states toward calmer Alpha- or Theta-associated patterns.

VAGAL & AUTONOMIC REGULATION

The Body's Stress Off-Switch

Slow, calming sound and breathing may support parasympathetic activity and relaxation, often associated with reduced stress responses.

VIBRATIONAL FLUID DYNAMICS

Cellular Wave Massage

Sound travels through air and body tissues, including fluids and bone. At appropriate intensities, vibration can feel like a gentle physical stimulus and may help ease muscular tension.

NEUROCHEMICAL SHIFT

Biochemical Reset

Deep relaxation can influence brain reward and stress systems. Sound-based relaxation may be associated with changes in endorphins, dopamine, serotonin and cortisol.
